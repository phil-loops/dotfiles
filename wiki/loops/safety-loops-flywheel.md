title: The flywheel
section: Safety loops
order: 120
project: phishtank-facets
lede: Disagreements become proposals, proposals become facets, facets improve policies, and the policy's remaining disagreements start the next turn.
---

    disagreements ──► proposals ──► facets ──► policies
          ▲                                       │
          └──── the new policy's disagreements ◄──┘

1. **Disagreements.** A daily job compares each judgment with the policy's decision and records where they differ, missed or wrongly blocked, grouped by content fingerprint so one template counts once. Reviewer flags on readings and trusted facts are applied the same day.
2. **Proposals.** For a group no same-day fix explains, contrast statistics and a model each propose one narrow question that separates it from look-alikes the policy got right. Verdicts and duplicates are rejected.
3. **Facets.** A proposal is prototyped, backfilled over the recorded log and kept only if a fitted policy improves with it on held-out teams and on mail recorded after the proposal. A person approves.
4. **Policies.** Candidates are fitted (weighted and tree), replayed against production and v0, watched live in shadow for a week, and promoted per touchpoint by a person.

**Speeds.** Same day: trusted facts and reading corrections. Weekly: refit weights and cutoffs. Each turn: new facets.

**People** judge mail (most of the work, roughly 30 to 60 judgments a day once settled), approve facets, promote policies and set the tolerance for wrongly blocked mail.

**It is working when** the disagreement rate on randomly sampled allowed mail keeps falling. Facets whose weight stays near zero are retired.
