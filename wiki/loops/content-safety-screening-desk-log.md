Continues [[content-safety-screening-desk]].

**The exception slip.** A reviewer can waive one named rule for a message, an account, a domain, or a template family, with a reason and an expiry. Every other rule keeps running. A reviewer can also correct a note, or record that this agency sends for that client, and the rules run again.

**The log.** Every decision keeps the evidence and the rules used at the time; later findings are new entries, never edits. Changes are tested on cases that did not inspire them, with the notes covered. The old clerk stays on as an instrument until the notes beat it.

**The handover.** The new desk first records what it would do beside the old one without stopping anything. It takes over one kind of send at a time, with a way back, once its results justify it and it can handle the messages it asks to wait.

**What the desk is for.** It is not protecting the sender. One bad message out the door costs every account that shares the same delivery reputation, so the desk protects everyone else in the building.
